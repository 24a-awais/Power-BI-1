Active Focus Campaign

Dignified Housing Reconstruction — Buner & District Bajaur

Following severe flash floods, temporary aid falls short. Under signed Memorandums of Understanding (MOU) with regional committees, our engineering pipelines are systematically replacing destroyed mud structures with highly permanent, climate-resilient brick houses in high-risk zones like Mamond Valley.
